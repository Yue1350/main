TTS Bot Overview

Welcome to TTS Bot! This Discord bot reads text messages in voice channels using Google Cloud TTS and provides various useful channel utilities.

Key Features

  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): Reads chat messages instantly in your current voice channel using Neural Google TTS voices.
  • Join & Leave Announcements: Plays friendly TTS audio notifications when users enter or leave a voice channel.
  • Abbreviation Expansion: Automatically converts internet slang and abbreviations (e.g., "ㅎㅇ", "ㄳ", "ㅇㅋ") into spoken words.
  • Custom Emoji Processing: Converts custom server emojis into full-size images and relays them via Webhooks.
  • Voice & Speed Control: Customizable voice options (Female/Male) and playback speeds ranging from 0.25x to 2.0x.
  • Auto Disconnect: Automatically leaves the voice channel when no human users remain.

Bot Commands

  • /입장 : Summons the bot to your current voice channel.
  • /퇴장 : Disconnects the bot from the voice channel.
  • /tts설정 : Opens a menu to select TTS voices, playback speed, and designated reading channels.
