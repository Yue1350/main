TTS Bot Overview

Welcome to TTS Bot! This Discord bot reads text messages in voice channels using high-quality Typecast AI Voices and provides advanced voice customization along with server management tools.

Key Features

  • Typecast AI Text-to-Speech: Reads chat messages dynamically using Typecast's natural AI voice models.
  • Advanced Voice Controls: Fine-tune your experience with customizable voice options ( 찬구, 주원, 채린, 등 ), playback speed (0.5x – 2.0x), audio pitch, and emotion presets (happy, sad, angry, whisper, etc.) with adjustable intensity.
  • Join & Leave Announcements: Automatically plays TTS voice alerts (e.g., "[Name] 어하", "[Name] 어바") when users join or leave the voice channel.
  • Text Normalization: Automatically converts internet slang (e.g., "ㅎㅇ", "ㄳ", "ㅇㅋ"), numbers to Korean words, English/usernames to Korean pronunciations, and handles URLs appropriately.
  • Custom Emoji & Media Handling: Converts custom server emojis into full-size images relayed via Webhooks and announces incoming attachments/stickers via TTS.
  • Dedicated Channel System: Effortlessly create, set, or clear dedicated TTS text channels.
  • Auto Disconnect & Auto Delete: Automatically leaves the voice channel when empty and cleans up temporary channel messages.

Bot Commands

  • /입장 : Summons the bot to your current voice channel.
  • /퇴장 : Disconnects the bot from the voice channel.
  • /tts설정 : Opens an interactive menu to configure TTS voice, speed, pitch, emotion, and emotion intensity.
  • /tts채널 [생성/지정/해제] : Creates a new TTS channel, sets the current channel as TTS-dedicated, or resets channel settings (Requires Manage Channels / Admin permission).
